Features

Everything you need to launch — nothing you don't

Nine reusable sections, thoughtfully composed. Each block is commented in the HTML so you can find, edit, or remove it in seconds.

Nine ready-made sections

Nav, hero, trust strip, features, how-it-works, pricing, FAQ, final CTA, and footer — all wired up and responsive.

One-file retheming

All colors, fonts, spacing, and radii live in css/variables.css. Change your brand palette without hunting through dozens of files.

Mobile-first by default

Designed at 390px first: 20px gutters, 44px tap targets, hamburger nav, and layouts that scale up gracefully to desktop.

Zero build step

Plain HTML, CSS, and a touch of vanilla JS. Open in any editor, push to Cloudflare Pages, and you're live. No webpack, no drama.

Original SVG visuals

Every illustration, logo mark, and avatar is original — no stock photos, no stolen assets, no fake testimonials with real people's faces.

Commercial license included

How it works

From download to deployed in three steps

No framework lock-in. No config files. Just edit the HTML, tweak the variables, and push.

Download & unzip

Grab the kit, extract the folder, and open index.html in your browser. Everything works locally — no server needed.

Customize your brand

Edit css/variables.css for colors and type. Replace the demo copy in index.html with your product's story.

Deploy & launch

Push to GitHub and connect Cloudflare Pages. Your landing page goes live in under a minute — SSL included, CDN included.
